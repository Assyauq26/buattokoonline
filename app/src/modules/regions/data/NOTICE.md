# Administrative region reference

The compressed snapshot contains public administrative reference data, not seller
or customer data. It is normalized from Emsifa API v2. Retrieval time and SHA-256
are recorded in `manifest.json`.

Emsifa declares MIT licensing and permits redistribution with source attribution:
https://github.com/emsifa/api-wilayah-indonesia#lisensi

Underlying administrative names/codes and postal data: Cahya DSN,
https://github.com/cahyadsn/wilayah and https://github.com/cahyadsn/wilayah_kodepos.
The upstream copyright and MIT terms are retained in `LICENSE.cahyadsn`.
The snapshot is not covered by a claim of exclusive ownership under Apache-2.0.
